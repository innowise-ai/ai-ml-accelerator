What goes into the prompt is also constrained: a handful of examples, spanning at least one clear
Pass and one clear Fail, with borderline cases carrying the most teaching value.

Pass if the answer gives concrete guidance on which examples to pick — a small number (roughly 2-8,
not dozens), covering both verdicts, with some attention to clear versus borderline cases.

Fail if the answer gives no selection guidance beyond "pick representative ones", or recommends
loading in tens of examples.
