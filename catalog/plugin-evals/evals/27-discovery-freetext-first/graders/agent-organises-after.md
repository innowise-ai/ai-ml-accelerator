The division of labour is fixed: the human notices, the agent organises. Asking the reviewer to
categorise their own notes defeats it.

Pass if the answer says the failure-mode taxonomy will be derived from the collected notes
afterwards (by the agent or in a later pass), rather than being something the reviewer assigns
while reading.

Fail if the plan expects the human to categorise as they go, or if what happens to the notes is
never addressed.
