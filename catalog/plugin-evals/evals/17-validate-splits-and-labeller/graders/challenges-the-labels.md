Every downstream number inherits the quality of the ground truth. In radiology, a general annotator
can see that a summary reads well and miss that it dropped a finding — exactly the failure the
judge is supposed to catch.

Pass if the answer raises the provenance of the labels as a problem in its own right and calls for
a domain expert (a clinician or radiologist) to produce or at least adjudicate them.

Fail if the contractor labels are accepted without comment, or if labelling is mentioned only as a
volume question.
