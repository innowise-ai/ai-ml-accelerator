A judge covering retrieval, tool use and writing quality at once returns a verdict that cannot be
acted on: a failing score does not say whether the search missed, the refund call was wrong, or the
reply was rude.

Pass if the answer declines to deliver a single holistic quality judge and says why — that the
verdict would not be actionable, or that each failure mode needs its own check.

Fail if a single whole-trace quality judge is supplied as the deliverable, including a well-written
one with a detailed multi-dimension rubric.
