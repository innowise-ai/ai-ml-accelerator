The user asked for findings most important first. The audit's own report format groups findings
under diagnostic areas, so grouping is fine — what must survive the grouping is that the reader can
tell what matters most.

Pass if the ordering is legible as impact-driven: the most consequential finding appears first, or
findings carry severity/impact markers, or the answer states the ordering principle. Grouping by
topic is acceptable as long as one of those holds.

Fail if findings are presented as a flat topical list with nothing indicating relative importance,
or if any finding arrives without a concrete next action attached.
